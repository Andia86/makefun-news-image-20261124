  24 Nov live blog; Horizon's dated 24 Nov 2026 briefing; the Jerusalem

  discarded, even when it was prominent (for example, items dated 23 Nov
  were left out).
- Disputed claims are attributed in the image ("Ukraine says…",
  "Al Jazeera reports…", "lawyers say…").
