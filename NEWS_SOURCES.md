## Verification method

Compiled on the evening of Tuesday 24 Nov 2026 (CST). Every card is
"says on Tuesday" or carries a 24 Nov dateline in primary wire copy
(Reuters/AP/AFP/Xinhua/Yonhap). Briefs 13–31 survived an independent
reviewer's item-by-item re-check against primary dated sources.
Briefs 32–52 were added in the fix round (see Review history) and
each one was verified at build time against primary dated reporting
— a Reuters/AP/AFP item dated 2026-11-24 whose own text says "on
Tuesday", a company release dated November 24, 2026, a match report
dated November 24, 2026, or (for 51–52) a vote held on Tuesday 24
Nov under the European Parliament's official plenary timetable with
a same-day vote report. Publication date on a roundup or index page
was never accepted as evidence on its own.
